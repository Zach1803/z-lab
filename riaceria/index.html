Ria Ceria Newsletter

News from the restaurant. Older issues unlock as our archive is restored.

Issue 1 · March 1987

Grand opening in Ipoh!

Taman Ria Ceria opens its doors on Jalan Sultan Idris with the first animatronic band in Perak. Pak Tenuk, Kak Kenyalang and Mak Orang perform every hour, and the brand-new Kebun Kancil play garden is open for kids under 10.

Issue 19 · August 1991

Kebun Kancil closes for maintenance

Following an incident during a birthday party on 14 August, Kebun Kancil will close until further notice. Si Kancil will remain behind the curtain and has been disconnected from the show controller. Staff report it is still moving at night. We thank parents for their patience.

Survive Night 1 to restore this issue
Issue 31 · June 1993

New night security policy

After two night guards resigned in one week, management reminds staff the band is set to "free roam" after closing because their servos seize if they stand still overnight. They will try to greet anyone still in the building. Do not let them greet you.

Survive Night 3 to restore this issue
Internal memo · not for publication · 1995

Re: the orangutan

Mak Orang has not been on the show schedule since 1993. Nobody has powered her on. She is still on the stage every morning, facing a different way. If the generator fails, do not wait in the dark for her to stop singing.
