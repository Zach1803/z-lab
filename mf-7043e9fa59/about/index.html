Three decades of keeping Malaysian businesses running

Mfinity Technologies Sdn. Bhd. was incorporated on 28 July 1997. We began as a wholesaler of computer hardware, software and peripherals, and grew into a digitalisation systems integrator focused on AI-driven IT, system maintenance and IT outsourcing.

Then and now

1997: supplying the hardware

We started by supplying computers, software and peripherals to Malaysian businesses. Installing and supporting that equipment taught us how our clients actually work, and what goes wrong when IT is treated as a box to buy rather than a system to run.

Today: running the whole system

We now design, build, secure and manage complete IT environments, from Microsoft 365 and Copilot to firewalls, private clouds and full data centres. Our engineers hold certifications from Cisco, Microsoft, Kaspersky, Lenovo and Dell, and we partner with the brands our clients depend on.

What has not changed

We still build long relationships, give each client a team that knows their setup, and recommend what fits their business rather than what is easiest to sell.

Our vision

To empower businesses through progressive technology, helping them grow and stay sustainable in a digital world.

Our mission
  • Deliver innovative, reliable IT that supports operational excellence and digital transformation
