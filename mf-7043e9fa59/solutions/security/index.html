Firewall and endpoint security

A next-generation firewall checks everything entering and leaving your network, while endpoint protection guards every laptop, desktop and phone against malware and ransomware.

What you get

  • Stronger first line of defence
  • Threats stopped before they spread
  • Lower risk of costly downtime
  • Easier compliance with regulators

What is included

  • Next-generation firewalls
  • Endpoint detection and response
  • Real-time threat detection
  • Automatic isolation of infected devices
  • Managed updates

Network security

Layers of protection that keep your network confidential and available, with continuous monitoring to catch unusual activity early.

What you get

  • Attacks detected in progress
  • Only the right people reach the right systems
  • Data protected in transit and at rest

What is included

  • Intrusion detection and prevention
  • Secure remote access
  • Network segmentation
  • Encryption
  • Vulnerability assessments

Penetration testing

Certified testers attack your systems the way a criminal would, then show you exactly what they found and how to fix it, in order of risk.

What you get

  • Know your weak spots before attackers do
  • Evidence for auditors and the board
  • A prioritised fix list, not just a scan report

What is included

  • External and internal network tests
  • Web application testing
  • Wireless network testing
  • Detailed report with remediation advice
  • Re-test after fixes

Data loss prevention

Stop sensitive information leaving the organisation by accident or on purpose, whether by email, USB drive or cloud upload.

What you get

  • Customer and staff data kept private
  • Support for PDPA compliance
  • Earn and keep customer trust

What is included

  • Data classification
  • Policy-based blocking and alerts
  • Encryption of sensitive files
  • Monitoring and incident reports
