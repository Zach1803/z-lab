How a project runs

  1. Discover

    A workshop with your teams to list the tasks that cost the most time, and rank them by value and risk.

  2. Prepare

    Clean up permissions, data sources and licences so AI only sees what it should.

  3. Pilot

    A small group uses the solution for four to six weeks while we measure the time saved.

  4. Scale

    Roll out to the wider organisation with training, policies and ongoing support.
