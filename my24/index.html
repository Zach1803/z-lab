    How this was made

    The map is NASA's Blue Marble (December 2004) draped over GEBCO relief, with Black Marble 2016 night lights, lit by the sun's real position for each time on today's date. Sunrise and sunset times are calculated live for where you see them. Arcs, streams and sparkles on the map are illustrations, not tracked ships, trains or births.

    Daily figures: 2025 annual totals ÷ 365, rounded. Population is DOSM's 2026 estimate.
