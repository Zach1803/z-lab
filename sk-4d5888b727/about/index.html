Sin-Kung Logistics was incorporated in Malaysia on 25 October 1994. We are an integrated logistics provider focused on airport-to-airport road feeder services, alongside container haulage, warehousing and distribution, customs brokerage and forwarding, express delivery and cargo escort.

Our aviation arm, Sin-Kung Airways, operates Boeing 737-400 freighters and a Piaggio P180, and runs a maintenance hangar in Langkawi.

On 1 August 2022 the company became Sin-Kung Logistics Berhad, and on 15 May 2024 it listed on the ACE Market of Bursa Malaysia.
