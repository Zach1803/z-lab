Our core business

Road feeder services: air freight by truck.

When a flight's cargo needs to reach another airport, we take it by road under customs bond, on full or shared truckloads. Scheduled runs link the main gateways, and we run ad-hoc trucks to any airport on request.

  • Domestic: KLIA, Subang and Penang International Airport.
  • Cross-border: Singapore Changi and airports in Thailand.
  • Full or less-than-truckload, including refrigerated box trucks and trailers.
  • Permits and customs handled for you, including duties and taxes on cross-border moves.
