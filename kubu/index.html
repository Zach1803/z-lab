    Incident response

    The first hour, minute by minute.

    When ransomware hits, the first hour decides how bad the next month is. This is what happens after you call our hotline.

    T+0

    You call. A human answers.

    No ticket queue. An on-call incident lead takes the call, confirms scope and opens a bridge with your IT team.

    T+5

    Affected machines are isolated.

    Through our EDR we cut infected hosts off the network while keeping them powered on, so the evidence survives.

    T+15

    Accounts are locked down.

    Compromised credentials are reset, sessions revoked and MFA enforced. Attacker footholds in M365 or Google Workspace are removed.

    T+30

    We find patient zero.

    Forensics traces how they got in, what they touched and whether data left. You get a first written situation report.

    T+45

    Backups are verified clean.

    Before anything is restored we check the backups for persistence, then plan recovery in business-priority order.

    T+60

    Reporting obligations are lined up.

    If personal data or critical systems are involved, we prepare the notifications with your legal team so nothing is late.
