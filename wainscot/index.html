45-G · under the arches

Follow the blue script and the pole.

The shop sits behind an arched five-foot way. There's a white iron bench and a wooden one for whoever is waiting, a board by the door, and the pole keeps turning beside the arch.

Reception

Say hello at the counter.

Pomades and clays wait in the glass case. Pay at the terminal or scan the QR. The crest behind the counter reads Cuts n Shave, est. 2020.

A wainscot is the panelling on the lower half of a wall. Look around: it runs across every wall in here.

The white frame

Step through the frame.

The menu is printed straight onto the glass beside it: cuts, beard work, washes, perms, keratin and scalp treatments.

The floor

Four mirrors, four chairs.

White frames on navy walls, a basin at every station and horn lamps glowing orange overhead. Capes are black with gold scrollwork. A haircut and style is booked for an hour, so nobody rushes your fade.

The back of the shop · Academy

A number 9 racer by the stairs.

A white vintage Suzuki racer is parked at the foot of a steep steel staircase strung with fairy lights. Behind it is the glass room, with the mezzanine above.

Want to learn the trade? Ask at the counter about the next Academy class.
