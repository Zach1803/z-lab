Cara bermain

How to play congkak

  1. Each player owns the seven kampung (holes) on their side and the rumah (big store) on their left. Every kampung starts with seven buah.
  2. On your turn, scoop up every buah from one of your kampung and drop them one by one, going clockwise. You drop into your own rumah as you pass it, but skip your opponent's.
  3. Last buah lands in your rumah? Go again.
  4. Lands in a kampung that already has buah (either side)? Scoop them all up and keep sowing from there.
  5. Lands in an empty kampung on your side? Tembak! You capture it plus everything in the kampung directly opposite.
  6. Lands in an empty kampung on their side? Mati: your turn ends.
  7. When a player has nothing left to sow, the other keeps whatever is still on their side. Most buah in the rumah wins.
