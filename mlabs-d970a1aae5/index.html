Who we are

Your choice of solution partner, always.

Mlabs began in 1992 as Multimedia Research Lab, born from a software project at Universiti Sains Malaysia. Its first product, MCS, brought IP-based video conferencing to Malaysian organisations.

Incorporated in 1997 and listed on the ACE Market of Bursa Malaysia in 2005, the company has grown into a diversified holding group. Our businesses now span enterprise IT and cybersecurity, Shariah-compliant financing, trading software, distribution, training, marketing and property.

What connects them is a simple promise: listen first, then deliver solutions that just work.
