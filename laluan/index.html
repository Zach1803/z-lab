Stations

Every station, step-free.

All stations have lifts from street to platform. Search for one to see its facilities, or tap it to find it on the map.

Accessibility

Built for everyone who travels.

If you need a hand, tell us before you travel and a member of staff will meet you at the gate and help you onto the train.

Lifts everywhere

Two lifts at every station, so there is always a backup if one is under maintenance.

Level boarding

Platform edges line up with the train floor, with no gap to step over.

Tactile paths

Guidance paving from the entrance to the platform, and Braille on every lift button.

Travel assistance

Book help up to 24 hours ahead in the app, or press a help point at any station.
