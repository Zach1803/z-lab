The idea

A tower that grows out of a park, not a car park.

Halia means ginger, the plant that thrives in the shade of the rainforest. We kept 78% of the site green, put the cars underground, and gave every home a balcony that wraps the building.

Every home has a view

Eight homes per floor, arranged so that no two balconies look into each other. Corner homes see two ways.

Cooler by design

Deep 1.7 m balconies shade the glass from the afternoon sun, and brass fins between homes cut the western glare.

Built to last

Freehold title, a 47th-floor sky garden for residents, and a sinking fund we publish every year.

Floor plans

Four layouts, all with a balcony that runs the full width.

Plans are drawn to scale. Sizes are built-up area including the balcony. Every home comes with two car parks, three for corner homes and penthouses.

Facilities

A resort on level seven, a garden on level forty-seven.

Two floors of shared spaces that we designed first, before the homes, because they're where the building becomes a neighbourhood.
